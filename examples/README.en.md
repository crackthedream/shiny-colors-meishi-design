# Two anonymized design examples

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

These pairs show two different ways to derive composition from a selected card illustration. The producer name is the generic Chinese label “示例制作人” (Sample Producer), and contact information is omitted entirely. The previews are unofficial layout studies containing third-party game illustrations. Editable templates contain no illustrations, signatures, unit logos or font binaries. See [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for sources and licensing boundaries.

## 01 · Flowing summer

![Anonymized Hiori summer card, front and back](flowing-summer/preview.png)

- **Visual evidence:** The reaching hand, wind-swept hair, open sky and diagonal railing in Hiori Kazano's 【耿耿】.
- **Front:** A diagonal name panel follows the image's movement. The title sits in the sky; the face, hands and gaze determine the available negative space.
- **Back:** Cyan and mint bands translate the direction of wind. Curves of different weights, generous cream space and open typography organize the reading sequence.
- **Reusable decision:** Identify the image's movement before choosing an information-panel shape. Choose another composition when another illustration offers different cues.
- **Editable templates:** [Front SVG](../skills/shiny-colors-meishi-design/assets/templates/flowing-summer/front.svg) · [Back SVG](../skills/shiny-colors-meishi-design/assets/templates/flowing-summer/back.svg)
- **Sources:** [Official character page](https://shinycolors.idolmaster-official.jp/idol/illuminationstars/hiori/) · [Official card image](https://shinycolors.idolmaster-official.jp/assets/img/idol/illuminationstars/hiori/card/card_1.png)

## 02 · Dress bookplate

![Anonymized Rinze dress card, front and back](dress-bookplate/preview.png)

- **Visual evidence:** The blue dress, cream lace and escalator diagonals in Rinze Morino's 【階段式純情昇降機】.
- **Front:** The name panel's sloping lower edge follows the escalator. A vertical title sits beside the dress, giving the information a deliberate relationship to the image.
- **Back:** Fine blue-and-cream borders, a small floral ornament and the prominent title “純情” establish the composition. Clothing cues become border proportions and decorative rhythm rather than a large diagram of the skirt.
- **Reusable decision:** Extract a small number of costume details, then use spacing, proportion and typography to develop a bookplate-like layout.
- **Editable templates:** [Front SVG](../skills/shiny-colors-meishi-design/assets/templates/dress-bookplate/front.svg) · [Back SVG](../skills/shiny-colors-meishi-design/assets/templates/dress-bookplate/back.svg)
- **Sources:** The [official costume page](https://campaign-shinycolors-song-for-prism.idolmaster-official.jp/283prostyle/collection/rinze-kaidanshiki/) cross-checks the card title and costume. The illustration used in this example was obtained from the [community card database](https://imassc.gamedbs.jp/chara/show/12/429), specifically its [archived image](https://imassc.gamedbs.jp/image/card/still/1621490796006_yqxaof96.jpg). The community domain is not an official host.

## Verification scope

Each public preview is a **1600 × 608 px** front-and-back comparison, not a high-resolution illustration asset or print file. The two previews and four templates were checked for text content, glyph coverage and safety bounds, then rendered individually to review anonymized names, spacing and graphic placement. Templates embed no images, fonts, external resources or personal contact fields.

The repeatable part is the research, decision-making, revision and delivery process. These examples demonstrate design decisions; they do not prescribe diagonals or fine borders for every future card.
