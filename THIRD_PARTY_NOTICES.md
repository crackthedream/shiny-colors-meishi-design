## Fonts / 字体

The previews were rendered using the following open font projects. No font binary, subset or embedded font is distributed in this repository. Templates contain family aliases only; users may install the indicated fonts or choose substitutes and recheck layout. Obtain fonts and their current license files from their projects rather than extracting them from previews.

预览使用下列开放字体项目渲染。仓库不分发字体文件、子集或嵌入字体；模板仅保留字体别名。使用者可从项目获取字体和当前许可文件，或选择替代字体并重新核对版面。

- [Noto CJK](https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk): Noto Serif SC Regular / Medium; Noto Sans SC was used for the preview-sheet labels.
- [Shippori Mincho](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Shippori+Mincho): SemiBold.
- [LXGW WenKai](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai): Regular.
- [Cormorant Garamond](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cormorant+Garamond): Light Italic, rendered from a static instance.
- [Source Han Sans](https://github.com/adobe-fonts/source-han-sans): Japanese Normal.
- [Kaisei Tokumin](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Kaisei+Tokumin): Bold.

The font projects use their own licenses, commonly SIL Open Font License; the repository's MIT license does not replace those terms. The exact alias/weight mapping for the templates is listed in the [English example guide](examples/README.en.md#editing-and-export) and [中文示例说明](examples/README.zh-CN.md#编辑与导出).
